📖 How to Play Roti Share

1

🔪 Slice the Roti

Choose how many equal pieces you need, or preview the cut line. Slicing equally creates unit fractions like 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 1/6, 1/8.

2

✋ Share to Plates

Click and drag 3D pieces to Chintu's, Meera's, or Golu's plate. Fair sharing means everyone gets the same mathematical fraction!

3

🔄 Rotate the 3D Camera

Drag with mouse or touch to inspect the table from any angle. Use the scroll wheel to zoom into the roti and characters.

4

🌟 Master Fraction Concepts

Explore fair sharing, unit fractions, comparing sizes (1/2 > 1/4), equivalent fractions (1/2 = 2/4), and adding portions!
